## Free workflow (no paid plans, all on a phone)

1. **Get the files.** Open the ElevenLabs canvas, tap each "E1 S…" image node, and download the full-size image.
   In ElevenLabs › Text to Speech › History, download Bernie's and Gabby's Day 1 lines.
2. **Animate each shot** in a free image-to-video app that gives free daily credits (for example Kling AI,
   Hailuo AI or the Meta AI app; free allowances change, so check the app). Upload the keyframe as the
   start image, set 9:16 and 5 seconds, and paste the motion prompt below. Do one or two shots a day if
   credits run out.
3. **Edit in CapCut (free).** Put the clips in order, add the two voice files under them, and line the
   lines up with the table above. Add sound effects from CapCut's free library (phone buzz, thud, record
   scratch, splash), auto-captions, and the text below. Export at 1080×1920.
