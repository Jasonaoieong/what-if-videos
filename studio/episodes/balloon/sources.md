# Sources — water balloon from the Burj Khalifa
- Burj Khalifa 828 m.
- Model balloon: 10 cm sphere of water = 0.524 kg ("half a kilo"; latex mass ignored), sphere CD 0.47 (same convention as the bowling/melon episodes), assumed to stay intact and round in flight (a real balloon would wobble and flatten) → Vt 47.6 m/s at sea level.
- Sim (pipeline/sim.py, std atmosphere, 828 m): 170.1 km/h at 10 s; peak 173.8 km/h; impact 47.9 m/s = 172 km/h after 20.5 s; KE = ½·0.524·47.9² ≈ 600 J.
- Raindrops: 0.524 L / volume of a 4 mm drop (33.5 mm³) = 15,625 drops; 4 mm raindrop terminal velocity ≈ 8.8–9 m/s ≈ 32 km/h (Gunn & Kinzer 1949; same as rain episode). Total KE ≈ 15,625·½·3.35×10⁻⁵ kg·(9 m/s)² ≈ 21 J → ≈ 28× less ("about thirty times").
