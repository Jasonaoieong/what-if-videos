# Sources — what if Earth had no air: penny vs feather
- Vacuum fall from 828 m: t = √(2h/g) = 13.0 s, v = √(2gh) = 127.5 m/s = 459 km/h, identical for both objects (sim with drag off).
- Penny in air tops out ≈ 40 km/h — Louis Bloomfield via Scientific American (see penny episode sources). No number used for the feather in air.
- Black daytime sky without an atmosphere: blue sky comes from Rayleigh scattering by air; with no air the sky is black (as on the Moon).
- Apollo 15 (1971): 1.32 kg aluminium geological hammer and a falcon feather dropped together on the Moon, landed simultaneously — NASA NSSDC https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/apollo_15_feather_drop.html (astronaut not named in the video).
- Hypothetical scenario; people could not breathe — not addressed.
