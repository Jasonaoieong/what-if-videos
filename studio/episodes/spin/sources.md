# Sources — What if the Earth stopped spinning?
- Equatorial surface speed 465 m/s ≈ 1,674 km/h (2π·6378 km / 23.93 h).
- Speed of sound at sea level ≈ 1,235 km/h (343 m/s).
- Strongest measured tornado wind ≈ 484 km/h (301 mph), Bridge Creek–Moore 1999, Doppler on Wheels — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Bridge_Creek%E2%80%93Moore_tornado ; https://www.weather.gov/oun/events-19990503
- Ocean bulge ~8 km at the equator migrates poleward → equatorial megacontinent + two polar oceans; a day would last a year — W. Fraczek, Esri ArcUser: https://www.esri.com/about/newsroom/arcuser/what-would-happen-if-earth-stopped-spinning
- 70 Myr ago: 372 days/year, day ≈ 23.5 h (de Winter et al. 2020, Paleoceanography & Paleoclimatology) — https://www.space.com/ancient-clam-fossil-reveals-earth-shorter-day.html
- Simplification: speed at latitude = 1,674·cos(lat) km/h; time compressed for the oceans and day/night beats.
